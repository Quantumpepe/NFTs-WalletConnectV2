TurboPepe-AI NFT Program

The TurboPepe-AI NFT Program is a long-term access and utility framework designed to grow alongside the TBP-AI ecosystem. It is not a speculative NFT collection, not a short-term hype experiment, and not a promise of instant rewards.

At its core, the TurboPepe-AI NFT functions as a digital access key. Ownership unlocks future utilities, gated features, and participation layers that will be delivered through TBP-AI services.

The collection is structured into two tiers within the same universe. Both tiers share the same long-term vision and ecosystem alignment, while offering different levels of access and future utility depth.

Gold NFT represents the premium access layer. It is designed for supporters who want deeper integration into the TBP-AI ecosystem and priority access to advanced features.

Silver NFT serves as the entry tier. It provides access to core utilities and community features, keeping participation accessible for a wider audience.

These NFTs do not contain on-chain artificial intelligence. AI functionality is delivered through TBP-AI services, with the NFT acting purely as a permission and access layer.
